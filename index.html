CrowLogs — raid logs and Mythic+ leaderboard for Tauri WoW Legion

CrowLogs is a free, open-source logging site for the Tauri WoW Legion realm Evermoon, made by the Crows' Nest guild. It is a warcraft-logs style site for Tauri: raid rankings from uploaded combat logs plus a Mythic+ leaderboard. Drop a WoWCombatLog.txt file and CrowLogs reads every boss encounter, computes per-player damage, healing and fight duration, and builds per-boss leaderboards.

  • Per-boss DPS and HPS leaderboards by difficulty and spec
  • Player profiles with rankings, log history, talents and gear
  • Mythic+ leaderboard: the best key and highest score in every Legion dungeon, this week and all time
  • Mythic+ best runs and key history for every player, from the Tauri armory
  • Optional CrowLogsHelper addon to record your exact build per pull

Source code: github.com/ivkoneli/CrowLogs
