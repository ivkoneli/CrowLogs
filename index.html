CrowLogs — World of Warcraft combat log DPS & HPS rankings

CrowLogs is a free, open-source WoW combat log analyzer made by the Crows' Nest guild. Drop a WoWCombatLog.txt file and CrowLogs reads every boss encounter, computes per-player damage, healing and fight duration, and builds per-boss leaderboards.

  • Per-boss DPS and HPS leaderboards by difficulty and spec
  • Player profiles with rankings, log history, talents and gear
  • Mythic+ best runs and key history from the Tauri armory
  • Optional CrowLogsHelper addon to record your exact build per pull

Source code: github.com/ivkoneli/CrowLogs
